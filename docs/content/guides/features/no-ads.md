# Grindr client without ads

The official Grindr app puts massive amounts of advertisements in grid, direct messages, profiles and your every action. **[Open Grind](https://opengrind.org)** is the most popular unofficial Grindr client with 60k+ downloads. 100% free, open source, secure, no ads, no trackers. Developed independently and transparently, released under MIT license. Use Open Grind to avoid tons of advertisements from Grindr and browse grid freely.
