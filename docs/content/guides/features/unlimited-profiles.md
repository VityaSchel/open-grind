# Unlock infinite profile grid in Grindr

The official Grindr app makes it impossible to reach more than a dozen of people in the grid without a paid subscription. Additionally, even on these limited available profiles, free users are only able to unlock messaging for a limited number per day.

Open Grind unlocks all profiles in Grindr and allows you to browse grid without any limitations for free. With Open Grind the subscription is not required to message any profile, and you can even [open profiles directly by ID](/guides/features/command-center/open-profile-by-id/).

Open Grind is the most popular maintained unofficial Grindr client with 30k+ downloads. It's 100% free, open source, safe and secure, actively developed by independent community members from all around the world. There is no ads, zero trackers and no telemetry in Open Grind, its code is publicly available for anyone, licensed under MIT license and audited by humans. Open Grind prioritizes security, stability and correctness, focuses on stealth and eliminates any risk of getting account restrictions for using it, constantly updates with new features and is available for all platforms.
