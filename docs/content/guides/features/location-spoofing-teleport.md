# Location spoofing in Grindr

To set a custom location for your Grindr profile and teleport to a specified place on the map, use the built-in location picker in Open Grind client. This method does not require a paid subscription or GPS faking, and carries no risk to the account, since the Grindr API see the location natively, as if you were in that spot.

1. Open the Open Grind app
2. When prompted to select your preferred location for the "Browse" page grid, tap on "Choose manually" — you don't need GPS for this
3. In the opened panel with a map, tap any location in the world you'd like to appear in, for both your cascade grid and your profile for users in that spot
4. Alternatively, enter the preferred location name into the search bar and choose one of the results, or use the "locate me" button to quickly center the map on your current GPS location
5. Finally, tap on the "Save" button and you will be immediatelly and permanently teleported to the specified location for your Grindr profile only
